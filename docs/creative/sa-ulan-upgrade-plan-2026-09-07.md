# Sa Ulan Upgrade Plan — 2026-09-07

Balance at planning: **1,115 credits (Plus)**. v3 cost ~320 all-in. Ceiling for
this whole plan: **≤ 250 credits**, leaving 850+ for the next production.
Standing rules apply to every generation: the verbatim continuity block
(Jaz cream v-neck; Rafa plain black button-down, no jacket; venue line per
scene), `drama` genre + explicit no-smiling on hostile beats, HTML props for
anything readable, and a cost check before every batch.

**Strategy guardrail (D9, market deep-dive):** the film is top-of-funnel, not
the product. Photoreal-AI leads measure the lowest willingness-to-pay of any
format; the pilot's moat is human faces. So credits go to (a) removing flaws
that break immersion and (b) making the film work harder in the funnel —
never to making AI leads incrementally more perfect. If a tier's gate fails,
stop; the surplus belongs to the next production.

1. **S3a menu-standoff regen (22.5 cr)** — the one logged defect: Rafa in a
   denim jacket instead of the black button-down. Regen prompt is in the

**Gate:** v3.1 watched end-to-end on a phone. Only then consider Tier 2.

## Tier 2 — Funnel cut-downs: make the film sell itself (~0–50 cr)

The market playbook: the ad IS the first episode — hook clip, cut at the
cliffhanger, deep-link. We have 84s of finished footage and use none of it
off-site. Mostly editing, not generation:

4. **15–30s teaser cuts (0 cr, ffmpeg):**
   - *Hook cut:* cold-open standoff → "Bakit mo ginawa 'yun?" → SMASH TO
     BLACK → "Panoorin ang buong kwento — libre" + link.
   - *Verdict cut* (judgment post, the highest-comment register per the
     Reddit scoreboard): the shredder beat + "Tama ba ginawa ni Jaz? 👀"
     end-card — comments are the CTA, link in first reply.
5. **One new hook shot only if the cold open doesn't hold as a standalone
   ad** (~22.5 cr, same continuity block). Run the virality predictor on
   both cuts first — free sanity check — before deciding.
6. These cuts feed: FB page organic, the restarted PH ad push (pairs with
   the ad-03-bold-statement family), and the pre-order funnel's top.

**Gate:** teaser posted; if its view→site click-through beats the current
ad family's, it earns ad budget.

## Tier 3 — Optional polish, strictly capped (~45–70 cr)

7. **Restaurant-interior drift** (the "minor accepted drift"): pick the best
   of the three dinner shots, regen the other two with its venue block
   (2 × ~22.5 cr). Do this ONLY if Tier 1+2 leave the drift noticeable on
   phone playback — nobody has complained yet.
8. **Hero-shot upscale** (cold open, cradle finale) — get the cost first;
   skip if upscaling the two shots costs more than ~25 cr total. Full-cut
   upscale: not worth it for a funnel asset.

## Explicitly NOT doing

- **No full regen of the film at higher fidelity** — v3 is proof and funnel
  fuel; perfection spend works against the authentic-faces wedge.
- **No AI voice/dub pass.** v1's subtitle-only call stands: social-native,
  dodges lip-sync, and AI Taglish dubs are actively memed in-market right
  now (Linda Walker discourse). Revisit only with human VO.
- **No sequel/Kabanata generation inside this plan.** The ending's A/B/C
  vote data decides what gets made next (vote-data citation rule) — pull the
  `watch_vote` tally from the dashboard first, then plan that as its own
  production out of the remaining 850+ credits.
- No AI-generated readable text, ever (HTML props rule).
