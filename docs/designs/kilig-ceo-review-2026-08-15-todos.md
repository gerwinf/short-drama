## The one question

The deep-dive's investor attack ends in a single load-bearing uncertainty:
**who pays real money, and from which segment (PH mass vs diaspora)?** Every
open TODO is ranked by its contribution to answering that — with founder time
as the only scarce resource and no new spend until existing data is read.

### 10x Check
The 10x version of this backlog is not doing more items — it is noticing that
four items (diaspora funnel read, reserver interviews, pre-order threshold,
partner conversations) form one two-week evidence chain that answers the
money question with data already collected plus five phone calls, before a
single additional peso of spend. Everything else either feeds that chain,
waits on its result, or is a standing rule that costs nothing to adopt.

  UA treadmill) applies at every scale, including ours.
- No dashboard/tooling work (D16 stays deferred) — the money question is
  answered by a segment filter and five phone calls, not new software.
- No app-phase resurrection despite the deep-dive's owned-surface argument —
  premise 1 (evidence first) still governs; the off-platform loop is currently
  the bio-link + email list, and that is enough to run every P0/P1 test.
