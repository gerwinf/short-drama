# Corpus: Customer Voice (agent report, 2026-08-15)

Scope: Trustpilot (ReelShort ~200+ reviews, DramaBox, ShortMax, NetShort, GoodShort, DramaShorts), PissedConsumer (463 ReelShort reviews), ComplaintsBoard, BBB, JustUseApp App Store mirrors, Xolvie/Sikayetvar, press quoting users (AV Club, MIT Tech Review, Variety, Deadline, BuzzFeed, PH outlets). Reddit blocked to crawler — Reddit sentiment via secondary sources. 100+ reviews, 10+ complaint venues.

## 1. Complaint taxonomy (frequency × intensity)

### 1.1 Billing/cancellation/refund abuse — HIGHEST
Dominant 1-star theme everywhere. ReelShort ~1.4–1.6★ Trustpilot, 84% 1-star; PissedConsumer 80% unfavorable, "nearly all billing-related"; only 31% of ComplaintsBoard complaints resolved.
- "I cancelled on day 6 … yet they've taken 2 payments since this."
- "still charged for two months after I canceled… generic email response."
- "they sent me a screenshot … stated that I did not cancel properly and that their policy is to not refund."
- "You took $36.99 from me and gave me nothing in return and that's straight out fraud."
- DramaBox: "only bought top up coins, and have now been charged between $16.99 and $18.99 for eight weeks."
- ShortMax BBB: "fraudulent company that does not bill customers properly or allow them to unsubscribe"; $9.99/wk charges continuing after cancellation.
- Recurring unauthorized amounts: $30, $9.99, $4.99, $31.19, $36.95, one $386; BBB case $144 refunded "as a courtesy."
Pattern: WEEKLY auto-renew at $5.99–19.99/wk, trial→full-price conversion, cancellation friction, refund denial by policy. "ReelShort is safe, but ReelShort's billing UX is not."

### 1.2 Coin-economy dark patterns — VERY HIGH
a) **Auto-unlock/autoplay draining balances (single most-hated mechanic)** — ReelShort ships auto-unlock ON by default (buried in Wallet settings); DramaBox autoplay "doesn't pause at a coin wall, it charges through it"; "took all my coins even when I did NOT watch"; "waking up to an empty balance"; testers: autoplay burns a paid coin pack in ~8 minutes.
b) **Expiring coins** — "coins expire so you can't build a reserve"; purchased coins don't expire but earned/bonus coins do — discovered only after losing balances; "grind ads, close the app, come back and the balance is gone."
c) **Price rising mid-series / opaque math** — "Once you buy credits, the price for each show raises drastically"; coin costs escalate toward finales (50–60+/ep; FlexTV 100+); "cannot check episode costs before watching"; "$4.99 coin packs and only got 5 episodes out of each one."
d) **Re-locking / non-restoring purchases** — watched episodes lock again; balances vanish on reinstall/device switch.
e) **Double-dipping: subscription + coins** — "bought a DramaBox subscription and still have to buy coins"; DramaBox called "the most double-dipping."

### 1.3 Paywall resentment & total-cost shock — VERY HIGH
- "Watched a few free episodes, got hooked … Unlocking one series ran me almost $40."
- "The paywall hits right at the emotional peak every time."
- "A single binge night can quietly drain $50 to $100"; $40 in two hours on an 80-ep series.
- FlexTV "$20 lasting only an hour."
- GoodShort: episodes 70 coins, free earning caps ~40/day — free path designed to fall short.
- AV Club: "$0.30–0.50 per episode or $17–20 weekly"; finishing one series > a month of Netflix. "The cliffhanger is bait for a coin paywall."

### 1.4 Ads — HIGH
- Free tier: "ads relentless after every 30 second episode"; NetShort max 20 ads/day at 10 coins each while episodes cost more; "ad longer than the clip"; "ads every 20 seconds."
- Acquisition ads oversell: "watch free" ads that paywall; "bad acting and misleading clips."
- Broken reward ads: ground coins fail to credit — paid attention, got nothing.

### 1.5 Customer service — accelerant
"Responses to everyone's reviews are just bots"; no phone support; ShortMax "doesn't reply to emails"; My Drama automated responses. Turns billing disputes into "scam" reviews.

### 1.6 Story quality — MEDIUM-HIGH frequency, LOW intensity (tolerated)
- "Same old repetitive and recycled tropes … tropes are fine, execution is everything." Wall-to-wall billionaire CEOs, secret heiresses, contract marriages, werewolf exes.
- Endings: stories "just cut off and did not resolve the plot"; "no proper ending" despite 40 eps; money spent on hooks, not resolutions.
- NetShort recap-padding: 60-second episodes repeating previous content, burning paid coins.
- Viewers mock the writing while continuing to watch.

### 1.7 Translation/dubbing — MEDIUM
DramaBox complaints "cluster on machine-translation at scale"; ShortMax machine-translation frustrates VIP payers. Industry: "viewers won't continue if … the dubbed voice sounds robotic"; "click away and never return." Name mismatches audio-vs-subs recurring.

### 1.8 AI content ("AI slop") — FAST-GROWING, HIGH intensity
- AI titles 7% → ~40% of top charts within a year; China ~470 AI dramas/day; FlexTV halted all traditional shoots.
- "Uncanny valley"; early-2026 surveys: **photorealistic AI dramas registered the LOWEST willingness-to-pay of any AI content format** (MIT Tech Review).
- Flashpoint: ReelShort's AI-anime remake of queer hit *Poolboy* ("Under the Surface") — cast AND viewer protest; "big companies using AI to exploit creatives"; actors urge fans "not to watch AI versions of themselves"; cast member: "Even if half of the people cancel … they're still making money because they're so much cheaper to make."
- Fan blogs treat AI verticals as a distinct lower tier. Fans attach to ACTORS and real-life ships — exactly what AI removes.

### 1.9 Piracy as counter-move — MEDIUM, revealing
- 8+ mod-APK sites ("Unlimited Coins, Premium Unlocked, No Ads").
- Telegram channels aggregating full series; FB groups ("REELSHORT & DRAMABOX DRAMAS"); free-mirror sites.
- "How to watch free" guides rank top of search. The query volume is the customer voice: "the story is worth my time, not your price."

## 2. What users love
- "It's so addictive"; "top tier dramas."
- Cast chemistry & attractive leads; fans follow actors across shows; real-life ships (Seth Edeen + Nicole Mattox "cutest pair in the vertical world"; Jesse Morales fandom).
- Fits dead time/bedtime: 70% of My Drama viewers watch in bed; heavy viewers 13.1 hrs/wk (up from 9.5 five months prior).
- Instant emotional payoff: "insult, suffering, revelation and revenge before your tea gets cold"; ~80% cite plot attractiveness.
- Camp as feature: "like Hallmark movies, you just can't help wanting to watch"; "addictive, guilty, ridiculous, impossible to stop."
- Free-tier grinders praise honest ad-for-coins loops: "getting points/rewards relatively easy" — users happily pay in ATTENTION if the exchange rate is honest.
- PH: vertical drama maps onto teleserye DNA — "classic Pinoy afternoon teleserye in a 90-second window"; underdog-revenge (maid secretly the boss); Linda Walker 719-exam meme; **provincial Filipinas use reward-paying microdrama apps (Melolo etc.) to convert watch-time into GCash pesos — app as side-income** (VERA Files).

## 3. The gap: complain vs keep paying
- **Rating paradox:** DramaBox 4.8–4.9★ app stores vs ~2.0★ Trustpilot; ReelShort 4.6★/4.3★ stores, 100M+ downloads vs 1.4–1.6★ Trustpilot. In-store reviewers rate the CONTENT/high; Trustpilot/BBB rate the TRANSACTION.
- Complaints don't dent revenue: category $2.98B IAP 2025 (+115%); ReelShort $1B+/yr; US users 35.7 min/day > Netflix mobile 24.8. "Scam" reviews are written by people who FINISHED the series.
- Why: (1) cliffhanger converts anger into payment (Zeigarnik loop); (2) it's mobile gaming, not TV — coin abstraction, whale-tolerant pricing, dark-pattern defaults; users complain in TV terms, behave in gacha terms; (3) complaints segment by what's negotiable — tolerate tropes/cheese/subtitles (content), do NOT tolerate charges-after-cancel/disappearing coins/auto-unlock (CONSENT). Every "scam" review is a consent violation, not a taste violation; (4) exit ramps prove price sensitivity, not demand collapse — trust breaks → mod APKs/Telegram/YouTube, demand survives the billing boycott; (5) AI is the one complaint that threatens the CONTENT side — lowest WTP of any format, and it removes the parasocial actor attachment that powers 5-star reviews.
- **One-line synthesis:** "this app is a predatory rip-off and I can't stop watching" — the product (60-second dopamine melodrama with attractive human leads) has essentially no churn-predicting complaints; the monetization wrapper generates nearly all of them. **Strategic opening: honest billing on top of the same stories — they'd pay a fair price, they just refuse to be tricked into an unfair one.**
