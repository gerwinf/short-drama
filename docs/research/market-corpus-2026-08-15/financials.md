# Corpus: Financials & Unit Economics (agent report, 2026-08-15)

## 1. Market size and growth

**Global / ex-China:**
- Global in-app purchase (IAP) revenue from short-drama apps hit **$2.98B in 2025, +115% YoY** — the fastest-growing app category globally; Deloitte forecasts **$7–8B in 2026** (Appfigures/ASO World, Sensor Tower State of Short Drama Apps 2026). Q1 2025 alone was ~$700M, ~4x Q1 2024 (Sensor Tower).
- **Media Partners Asia (Aug 2026):** microdrama revenue **outside China grows from $2.7B (2025) to $3.6B (2026), reaching $9.5B by 2031**; US revenue more than doubles from $1.5B to $3.7B over the forecast; APAC ex-China triples to $2.4B (Screen Daily, World Screen).
- **Omdia:** global microdrama revenue (broader definition, incl. China/ads) ~**$11B in 2025 → $14B by end-2026** (36kr).
- Downloads: **850M in Q1 2026, +140% YoY**; in Q4 2025 short-drama downloads passed traditional streaming apps for the first time (733M vs 658M) (Sensor Tower, Appfigures); time spent grew +5.78B hours YoY in 2025.

**China domestic:** RMB ~63.4–67.8B in 2025 (CTDPIA vs iiMedia), **surpassing theatrical box office**; MPA: China microdrama $9.4B in 2025 → $16.2B by 2030; 830M+ viewers (Deadline/MPA, CNBC, trade.gov).

**Top markets:** US ~60% of global mobile short-drama revenue in 2024, then Japan (5%), Indonesia (3%); download growth now driven by SEA, LatAm, India while the US anchors revenue (Sensor Tower 2025/2026, CNBC).

**Concentration:** ~300+ apps active; **top 5 apps = 68.8% of tracked revenue** (12mo to Apr 2026), top 20 = 95%; but top 5 revenue apps drove only 28.8% of downloads (adjoe).

## 3. Mega Matrix (NYSE American: MPU) — FlexTV: cleanest English unit-economics disclosure

- FY2024 (20-F): revenue **$36.2M** ($31.6M membership/top-up + $3.7M ads); **UA expense = 62% of revenue**; 10.0M active users, 1.0M paying (10% conversion); **ARPU $3.15; ARPPU $31.22** (publicnow, SEC 6-K).
- FY2025: revenue $26.1M (-27.9%) after deliberately cutting UA; UA 62%→47% of revenue; ARPU $3.15→$3.42; adj. EBITDA loss $7.1M→$5.6M; cash $7.3M (StockTitan, Investing.com).
- Pivoted asset-light: licensed + AI-translated content (968 dramas + 10,500 multi-language titles, 16 languages), AI dubbing, Cayman redomicile, crypto treasury — a sub-scale player that couldn't make paid-unlock UA math work (StockTitan, TipRanks).
- Scale threshold read: at $26–36M revenue with 47–62% going to ads, never reached breakeven; profitability arrives near $1B scale with owned billing.

## 4. Dianzhong / StoryMatrix — DramaBox; ShortMax

- DramaBox 2024: revenue ~$323M, ~$10M net profit (~3%) — only major Western-facing platform openly profitable at scale that year; parent's overseas short-drama revenue >RMB 2B 2024 (Filmustage, 36kr, Wikipedia).
- Q1 2025 IAP $120M (+29%); cumulative global IAP $450M by Mar 2025; 50M+ MAU; **2025 Disney Accelerator** (Sensor Tower, Tracxn).
- Content: 2,000+ titles late 2024, mostly translated Chinese + ~60 US originals/yr from LA studio (AXIS AI Studios).
- **ShortMax (Jiuzhou):** 2024 net revenue $127M, +3,888% YoY; top markets **Indonesia/Thailand/Philippines**; strong Japan localization; supplies TikTok Minis + ByteDance PineDrama (AXIS, Antom). ReelShort + DramaBox + ShortMax ≈ 80% of overseas paid market 2024–25 (chinanews).

## 5. ByteDance's Hongguo (红果) — the free ad-supported disruptor

- 100M+ DAU by Jan 2026 (<3 years from launch), ~245M MAU Oct 2025, approaching 300M MAU 2026 (Pandaily, DaoInsights).
- 2025 revenue ~RMB 15–20B (~$2.1–2.8B), essentially all advertising (BigGo, Grokipedia).
- Free+ads took **>66% of Chinese market in 2025**, breaking paid-unlock. **Apr 2026: Hongguo cut producer rev-share from RMB 30–100 per 10k effective views to RMB 5–10 (-90%+)**; May 2026 eliminated minimum guarantees for AI content; leaked ~90% loss rate across domestic short-drama companies; top producers halted live-action shoots; rumored RMB 60B subsidy pool (36kr "Harsh Winter", 36kr subsidy piece, BigGo). Hongguo spun up e-commerce division.
- Overseas free-model export: **Melolo** (Nov 2024, SEA — +21x QoQ downloads Q1 2025, #6 SEA chart, 70% Indonesia) and Minishorts (US/EU) (KrAsia, SCMP, Vitrina). Paid model NOT yet undercut in US/Japan; free model owns China and is winning SEA downloads; incumbents adding ad tiers.

## 7. Key answers

- **UA share of revenue:** ReelShort ~55% (2025) → 44% (2028E); Mega Matrix 62% (2024) → 47% (2025); COL selling expense ≈65% of consolidated revenue 9M2025. UA + store fees historically consumed 75–85% of gross receipts (→ ReelShort's ~$1M net on ~$400M). Margin repair: web-store billing, ad tiers, franchise IP lowering CAC.
- **Content cost per series:** minor vs UA. China live-action RMB 300k–600k (up to 1.2M costume) per 60–100-ep series; US originals ~$150k–300k/series; translation/dubbing ~$30–100/ep/language (~5–10% of production cost); AI-generated as low as RMB 100–500/ep; premium AI series <RMB 200k vs RMB 1.5–3M live-action equivalent (World of Chinese, lens.streaming-radar, Sukudo, AIbase, 36kr).
- **Whale concentration:** 2–8% of users convert to payers; top 10% of payers = 40–60% of revenue; monthly ARPPU $15–35, whales $50–100+; blended ARPU $0.50–3.50 (adjoe, lens.streaming-radar, Mega Matrix).
- **Free vs paid:** China — free won decisively (>66%). Overseas — paid still compounding in US/Japan; ByteDance Melolo/Minishorts + TikTok PineDrama attacking SEA/LatAm with free playbook; incumbents hybridizing IAA+IAP.
- **AI economics:** COL's HK-IPO thesis is AI (script gen/translation, 1,000+ multilanguage titles); Mega Matrix credits AI translation for cost reset; ByteDance tools: one operator ~40 min/day releasable content at ~45% margin; AI short-drama ad placement +137% (May 2026); AI-drama market forecast RMB 24B 2026 — but Hongguo cut AI content from minimum guarantees = oversupply, rapid commoditization of low-end AI (guancha, hellochinatech, 36kr).
- **Structural summary:** UA-arbitrage business converging on 3 viable models: (1) scale paid platforms (ReelShort/DramaBox) profitable via owned billing + ads + franchises at ~$300M–1B revenue; (2) free ad/commerce platforms backed by traffic giants (Hongguo/Melolo) that crush producer economics; (3) everyone else squeezed out or pivoting to AI/licensing. MPA: microdrama "has begun to be an earnings story rather than a growth story."

Caveats: RMB→USD at ~7.2; ReelShort revenue differs MPA (net) vs Appfigures (gross); Hongguo figures are third-party estimates. Some primary sites (variety.com, sensortower.com, sec.gov) were proxy-blocked; figures via search-indexed excerpts.
