# Corpus: Competitor Landscape (agent report, 2026-08-15)

## Market snapshot
- Global short-drama IAP $2.98B 2025 (+115%), Deloitte forecast $7–8B 2026; Q4 2025 first quarter short-drama downloads (733M) beat traditional streaming (658M). Q1 2026: 850M+ downloads (+140%), ~$750M IAP (+20%) (Sensor Tower 2026).
- Ex-China ~$3B 2025 (≈3x YoY), US ≈ $1.3B; US = 60–70% of Chinese platforms' overseas revenue (Axios, MIT Tech Review). SEA/LatAm/India drive downloads; US anchors revenue.
- China: >RMB 50B, overtook theatrical box office 2025; 660–830M viewers.
- Concentration: top 5 apps 68.8% of revenue, top 20 = 95%; top 5 only 28.8% of downloads. ReelShort users average 35.7 min/day vs Netflix 24.8 (eMarketer).

## Per-company highlights
- **ReelShort** (Crazy Maple Studio, 49% COL, control lawsuit May 2026): ~400 LA-shot English originals/yr at ~$150–250K per 50–70-ep series; web-novel IP formula. Coins ~50–55/ep, finishing a series ≈ $37–47; weekly/monthly VIP; rewarded ads; own web store (≈half of margin expansion). $97M (2023) → $400M (2024) → $785M (2025) → ~$1.05B projected 2026 w/ first net profit (~$40M); 100M+ downloads; 1.34M cumulative ad creatives.
- **DramaBox** (StoryMatrix/Dianzhong): 2,000+ titles, mostly translated Chinese + ~60 US originals/yr. $323M rev / $10M net 2024; ~50M MAU; $74M gross billings Apr 2026 but 2026 net margin <1% — "growth without profit."
- **ShortMax** (Jiuzhou): translated library at volume; downloads strength Indonesia/Thailand/**Philippines**; revenue tilt US/CA/DE. $127M 2024 (+3,888%); supplies TikTok Minis + PineDrama.
- **DramaWave** (Kunlun Tech / Skywork AI): AI-native — >80% of new content AI-generated; $128M monthly billings Apr 2026; Skywork short-drama ARR >$700M by Q2 2026. Fastest scale-up ever in category.
- **SkyReels** (Kunlun): pure AI-generated dramas, SkyReels-V1→V4 models; US launch Dec 2025; part of Kunlun's $500M overseas commitment.
- **NetShort** (NETSTORY, SG, Jul 2024): translated + localized; heavy Meta UA; 130M+ downloads by Q1 2026; daily revenue up to $750K; #1 monthly revenue app Apr 2025.
- **GoodShort** (GoodNovel/Xinyue webnovel group): webnovel-IP adaptations; ~2M downloads, ~$11M rev/mo.
- **MoboReels** (MoboReader webnovel group): translated genre staples; 29M downloads; softening 2025.
- **FlexTV** (Mega Matrix, NYSE MPU): 968 dramas, 10,500 multi-language titles, 16 languages; $26.1M FY2025, adj EBITDA −$5.6M; AI production from Q2 2026. Only pure-play US public co.
- **Sereal+/FlareFlow/UniReel** (COL): Sereal+ Q3 2024 $13.45M net revenue, 710K MAU; UA nearly stopped mid-2025.
- **My Drama** (Holywater, Ukraine): local-language Western originals from My Passion book data; 85M+ users; revenue doubled 2025; record **$22M Series A (Jan 2026)** — largest microdrama round outside Asia. **Interactive AI character chats** (actors paid royalties up to $10K/mo); celebrity casting (Maksim Chmerkovskiy's Wild Silence; Hannah Stocking musical Playback).
- **Vigloo** (SpoonLabs, Seoul; $86M Krafton backing): Korean/Japanese/US originals (~200 in 2025, doubling); K-drama production values as differentiation; US = 50% of revenue.
- **Kalos TV / MiniShorts**: long-tail; MiniShorts ~$1M/mo on ~70K downloads — whale-efficient.
- **PineDrama** (TikTok/ByteDance, Jan 2026): licensed/partner content incl. ShortMax; free/ad-leaning + IAP; soft launch US/Brazil/Indonesia; **17.6–17.8M downloads in 30 days — #4 in category within 4 months**. The structural threat: TikTok UA firehose at zero marginal cost.
- **Melolo/PikoShow** (ByteDance, earlier): failed genre-market fit (shipped urban romance where locals wanted revenge/family drama); marketing ceased late 2025. Proof localization matters.
- **Hongguo** (ByteDance, China): 100% free ad-monetized; 100M+ DAU <3 years; 304M MAU Feb 2026 (+140M YoY); killed paid-coin model inside China.
- **Kuaishou** (Xifan + playlets): Kling AI ARR ~$240–300M; Kling 3.0 cut series cost from ~$200K to $7–14K.
- **Tencent** (WeChat Channels): distribution-not-app strategy.
- **Kuku TV** (Kuku FM, India): Hindi-first vernacular originals; 5M+ paying subs; local originals beat dubbed Chinese in India. India long tail: Flick TV, ReelSaga, Pocket TV, Amazon MX Fatafat, Zee Bullet, Balaji Kutingg…
- **US streamers**: Netflix Clips (Apr 2026), Prime Video vertical feed, Disney+ Verts, Peacock, HBO Max Shorts — vertical discovery feeds of existing IP, NOT coin-gated serialized verticals. Four Hollywood vertical studios launched in a single day in 2026.
- **New Western wave**: Holywater, AppReel etc.; celebrity/IP-driven; VC money arriving; format legitimizing.

## The standard playbook
1. **Serial structure + cliffhanger paywall**: 50–90 eps of 1–2 min; first 5–10 free; paywall lands on a cliffhanger ~ep 8–12 once viewer is emotionally committed.
2. **Coin economy tuned for whales**: ~50–55 coins/ep; packs $1.99–$50; finishing one series $37–47 (more than a month of Netflix); top series $200K–$1M+ unlock revenue each; subs $9.99–19.99/mo + weekly passes for the heaviest 10–20%; rewarded ads monetize non-payers.
3. **App-store bypass**: direct web stores avoiding the 30% cut — for ReelShort ~half of projected margin expansion.
4. **Saturation-level paid UA**: daily category ad creatives ~200 → 3,000+; leaders run 300+ creative variants/day across 4+ networks; ReelShort ~1.34M creatives cumulative; NewLeaf top-3 advertiser globally by volume alongside TEMU and TikTok. The ad IS the first episode: hook clip, cut at cliffhanger, deep-link.
5. **Localization pipeline** (3 rungs): AI subtitle/dub the Chinese back-catalog (120-min drama translated + dubbed in 4–5h at >95% accuracy); AI "re-skin" (same script re-rendered with different ethnicities/wardrobe/settings per market); local originals for top markets. Originals win the US; dubbed fills SEA/LatAm. Genre-market fit decisive (Melolo failure).
6. **AI production default in China, arriving overseas**: of ~128,000 micro-dramas launched in China Q1 2026, ~122,000 (95%+) AI-generated; ~470 AI titles/day Jan 2026; Kling 3.0: production 3–4 months → <1 month, ~$200K → $7–14K/series. Overseas AI-drama app market ~$650M expected 2026 (~6x YoY). Domestic China AI dramas ~90% loss rate → pushing everyone overseas. Live-action originals remain the premium/whale tier.
7. **Structural tensions**: (a) margin squeeze — DramaBox net margin <1%; (b) free ad-funded model (Hongguo domestic; PineDrama international) threatens coins the way free webnovels killed paid reading in China; (c) platform risk — TikTok sells the UA and now competes; (d) streamer entry = discovery feeds only, so far.

Caveats: estimates differ by methodology (IAP-only vs gross billings vs web-store); Hongguo RMB 150B figure contested; DramaWave/Skywork ARR company-reported.
