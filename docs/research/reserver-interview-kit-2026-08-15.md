# Reserver Interview Kit — P0/D2 (2026-08-15)

Purpose: 5 conversations with `reserve_click` users — the named, highest-intent
people the project has. Probes come from the market deep-dive: segment
(PH/OFW), faces-vs-dubs, coin-trap resentment, cancel triggers. Includes the
capture template (D3) and the optional mock-vote tail (D4).

Rules (same as the sprint): listen, don't pitch. No leading questions — the
qualifying signal is *unprompted*. Log verbatims in the language they were
said. One offhand comment does not count as a confirmed pain.

## 1. Recruiting

- **Who:** dashboard → CSV export → filter WTP = `reserved`, drop known-bad
  emails (Reachability section). Tag each PH / OFW / abroad-other from the
  location column before outreach — you want at least 2 abroad if they exist.
- **Target:** 5 completed; overbook to 7. If booking stalls after 4 days,
  offer ₱200–500 load credit (sprint precedent) — mention it only on the
  *second* touch, not the first.
- **Format:** 15–20 min video call, OR async voice notes (Messenger/WhatsApp/
  Viber) — lower friction for this audience and verbatims arrive pre-recorded.
  Async gets the same questions, sent one at a time, not as a wall of text.

## 2. Interview guide

Order matters: behavior first, then the reserve moment, then reactions.
Never name a pain before they do.

**A. Current behavior (5 min)**
1. "Kumusta! Ano'ng pinapanood mo ngayon — anong apps, anong oras ng araw?"
   (Listen for: ReelShort/DramaBox/NetShort/Melolo/FreeReels, FB/TikTok,
   teleserye, Vivamax. Frequency. Where they physically watch.)
2. "May binabayaran ka ba sa panonood ngayon? Saan napupunta ang pera —
   subscription, coins, load?" (Payment behavior, rails: GCash? card? load?)
3. **Linda Walker probe (A3 test):** "Napanood mo ba 'yung Linda Walker /
   'yung 719 exam scene? Ano sa tingin mo?" If yes: "Nakakapanood ka ba ng mga
   Tagalog-dubbed na Chinese drama? Okay lang ba ang dub, o iba pa rin ang
   totoong Pinoy?" — *this is the faces-vs-dubs question; do not ask it as
   "would you prefer authentic Filipino content" (leading).*

**B. The reserve moment (5 min)**
4. "Naalala mo noong nag-sign up ka sa Kilig — ano'ng pinindot mo bago 'yon,
   ano'ng inisip mo?" (Which surface: film/play/landing. What hooked.)
5. "Nag-reserve ka ng founding price. Bakit? Ano'ng inasahan mong makukuha?"
   (THE question. Let silence work. Verbatim everything.)
6. "May naalala ka bang presyo? Kung magbabayad ka na next week, okay pa rin
   ba, o may magpapabago ng isip mo?" (Price recall + cancel triggers —
   phrase as "ano'ng magpapabago ng isip mo", never "magbabayad ka ba talaga".)

**C. Reactions (5 min)**
7. **Coin-trap probe (blind spot 1) — indirect first:** "Sa mga app na
   ginagamit mo ngayon, ano'ng pinaka-nakakainis?" Only if they don't raise
   it themselves: "May nabanggit na iba tungkol sa coins/bayad — ikaw, may
   experience ka ba diyan?" (Unprompted coin/billing complaints = the
   qualifying pain. Prompted agreement = weak signal, log as prompted.)
8. **AI faces probe (D9):** show one Sa Ulan still + one real-actor teleserye
   still (neutral framing): "Dalawang klase ng production — may napapansin ka
   bang pagkakaiba? Mahalaga ba sa'yo kung totoong artista?" (Do NOT say
   which is AI first. Log whether they clock it, and whether they care.)
9. "Kung ikaw ang bumoboto kung ano'ng susunod na mangyayari sa kwento —
   gaano ka-interesado, honestly?" (Premise 4; score 1–5; watch for
   politeness inflation — the behavioral vote data outranks this answer.)

**D. Close (2 min)**
10. Referral ask: "May kakilala ka bang mahilig din sa ganito na pwede kong
    makausap?" (Names or handles — this rebuilds the PH network, D7.)
11. Thanks + what happens next. Do not promise launch dates.

**Optional tail — mock-vote stills (D4, only if under 20 min):** show the
2-minute "ano'ng gagawin mo?" cliffhanger stills; record which option and how
fast they answer. Directional only; never counts toward any gate.

## 4. Synthesis (after all 5)

One page in `docs/research/`, structured as: per-question verbatim clusters →
which deep-dive assumptions (A3, A5, A7) and premises (1–5) each cluster
supports or contradicts → changes proposed to the pre-order broadcast copy
(D5) and the next creative round (D10/D11). Listening cues carried over:
the Vivamax cue (log, don't act) and two new ones — unprompted "ayoko ng
coins/scam" language (supports anti-coin-trap positioning) and OFW
homesickness register ("piece of home") vs PH revenge register (informs
which POV the next creative round leads with).
